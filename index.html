How it works

Your money is compounded month by month, from today until the day you retire.

Every month
Your investment goes in, then the whole pot grows by one month's worth of your return.
Every year
Your salary rises by your raise, so from then on you invest a bigger amount each month.
Your return
All your assets are blended into one average, weighted by how much of each you hold.
Buying a goal
The money leaves the pot in the month you buy, so it stops growing from then on. That lost growth is usually the real cost of a big purchase — far more than the price tag.
If you fall short
You spend what you have and the rest is flagged as a shortfall. There is no borrowing in this model.
What it can't tell you
Real markets rise and fall; this uses one steady average. A bumpy portfolio is riskier than these numbers make it look.
Your inputs

Everything you can change, and what it does.

Current Savings
What you have already invested. Where the projection starts from.
Current Age
How old you are today.
Retire Age
When you stop investing. The gap between the two ages is your horizon.
Monthly Salary
Your take-home pay each month.
Invest %
How much of that pay you invest.
Invest Value
The same thing in rupees. Change either one and the other follows.
Salary Raise %
Your yearly raise. What you invest grows along with your pay.
Inflation %
How fast prices rise. This is what turns future rupees into today's money. Leave it at 0 to ignore it.
Fund / Asset Name
Just a label for the row.
% Split
How much of your money sits in that asset. Anything left unassigned is treated as cash and earns nothing.
% Return
What you expect that asset to earn each year.
Life Goal
Something big you plan to buy. Give today's price and the age you will buy it. A negative amount means money coming in instead.
Gold / USD Rate
Rupees per tola and per dollar. Used only for the two equivalent figures.
Your results

Everything the tool shows you, and how to read it.

Total Projected Wealth
Your pot when you retire, as a plain future number.
In today's money
That same pot, in what it could buy right now. This is the figure that actually means something.
You put in
Your own money, from start to finish.
Compounding
What growth added on top. Once this passes what you put in, time is doing more work than you are.
Goals take
What your planned purchases cost, at the prices they will be on the day.
Income /mo @ 4%
Roughly what you could spend each month in retirement without draining the pot.
USD / Gold Equivalent
The same wealth counted in dollars or in tolas of gold.
Final Year Salary
What you would be earning in your last working year.
Avg. Weighted Return
Your whole portfolio's yearly return, blended into one number.
Working Horizon
How many years of investing you have left.
The chart
Blue is the money you put in, green on top is growth. Dashed lines mark your goals. Hover anywhere to read that age.
Goal Cost on the Day
What each goal will really cost by the time you get there.
Portfolio Breakdown
Your final pot split across the assets you listed.
